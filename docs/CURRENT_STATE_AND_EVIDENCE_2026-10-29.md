## Hardware and research limits

The [current signal map](../manual/hardware-connections.md#current-m5-signal-map-for-the-ieee-report) records MPU6050 I²C2 on P0.10/P0.11 through alternative trainer contacts, TFT SPI0/GPIO routes and owner-reported SW2 ribbon behavior. It is a source/manual map; individual wire endpoints, connector choice, continuity, fitted module 5 V compatibility, pull-up rails and MCU-facing levels remain unmeasured. The owner reported best-score retention after a **board power cycle**. Neither the M5 nor M7 HEX's exclusion of flash sectors 14–15 proves retention through a later firmware programming operation; the Flash Magic erase mode and before/after sector contents were not archived.

The [original synopsis](Updated_ESDL_Synopsis_ML_IMU_Gesture_Recognition.docx) proposed four commands, real labeled recordings and detector comparison. The implemented M6/M7 tree classifies only left, right and neutral from one instantaneous calibrated X feature; automatic fire is still the game behavior. No flick-to-fire, adaptive difficulty, real-sensor accuracy, measured inference latency, code/data memory or power result is established. A physically working game does not establish classifier improvement. The [research plan](RESEARCH_PLAN.md) treats those questions as future experiments.

For professor submission, use the [final audit](FINAL_SUBMISSION_AUDIT.md) to identify the exact files being submitted, capture any remaining owner evidence and keep generated build output separate from intended source changes. Historical milestone records remain as written.
